> Snoopalot is the app that sends you a push notification every time your houseplants stop thinking about you, so you never miss a single moment of their indifference.

## Why it's the worst

Snoopalot solves a problem that does not exist for a user who cannot have it: houseplants do not think about you, and even if they did, you would not need a push notification about it. It takes the most pointless possible input — the interior emotional life of a fern — and delivers it with the urgency of a breaking-news alert, turning your phone into a live feed of botanical apathy. Nobody asked for it, nobody benefits from it, and its core feature is, by definition, always firing.
